Medical & Surgical Robotics

Yanxi Chen 陈沿希

I build and study medical robots—with the goal of making care safer and helping people live healthier lives.

I am an undergraduate in Intelligent Manufacturing Engineering at Tongji University (B.Eng. expected June 2027). I currently work with Prof. Yue Chen in the Biomedical Mechatronics (BM²) Lab at Georgia Tech.

Previously, I worked with Prof. Patrick Codd and Prof. Leila Bridgeman at Duke University, and with Prof. Peng Qi and Prof. Yichao Tang at Tongji University. These collaborations have shaped my interests in medical robotics, from device design and perception to autonomous planning and control.

My research spans autonomous endovascular navigation, robotic laser surgery, and language-guided surgical assistance. I connect robot learning, planning, and mechanical design with experimental validation on physical systems.

I love the journey from an idea to a working system: designing, building, learning from experiments, and improving it with a team.
